# Customizing Your Force Feedback

> Updated November 2026 (app 0.8.007). For a full walk-through, see the step-by-step tuning guide.

One advantage of an external force feedback app is that you can shape the feel to your taste. These are the main controls and what they change.

## 1. Understeer Effect
* **What you feel:** the steering goes light when the front tyres run out of grip.
* **Tuning:**
    * **Higher:** a clearer drop in weight when you push too hard.
    * **0%:** the steering keeps its weight even while the front slides.
* **Understeer Curve** changes how early and how sharply the weight drops.

## 2. Cornering Feel
* **What you feel:** the car's weight building in your hands as it leans into a corner.
* **Tuning:** higher values help you sense the car's balance and catch oversteer earlier.

## 3. Slide catching: SoP Self-Aligning Torque and Cornering Feel Boost (Slide)
* **SoP Self-Aligning Torque:** the wheel pulls in the counter-steer direction when the rear slides.
* **Cornering Feel Boost (Slide):** adds weight while you hold a slide.

## 4. Rotation Kick
* **What you feel:** a short, sharp kick the moment the rear starts to rotate. It is your earliest warning.
* **Activation Threshold:** raise it if kerbs trigger the kick too often.

## 5. Slide Texture
* **What you feel:** a gritty, sandpaper-like vibration when the tyres slide sideways.
* **Tuning:** raise the strength if it is too subtle, lower it if the wheel rattles.

## 6. Road Texture
* **What you feel:** kerbs, bumps and the road surface.
* **Tuning:** useful on wheels that feel numb or heavily damped.

## 7. Lockup Vibration, ABS Pulse and Spin Vibration
* **Lockup Vibration:** a judder when the wheels lock under braking.
* **ABS Pulse:** a rattle when ABS works.
* **Spin Vibration:** a hum that rises as the driven wheels spin up.

## 8. Min Force
* **What it does:** helps belt and gear wheels show small forces that their internal friction would otherwise hide.

## 9. Saving and sharing profiles

### Saving your own profile
1. Adjust the controls to your preference.
2. Type a name next to **"Save New"**.
3. Click **"Save New"**. Your profile appears in the profile list.

### Exporting to share
1. Select the profile you want to share.
2. Click **"Export Selected..."**.
3. Choose where to save the file. It contains all your settings and can be sent to other drivers.

### Importing a shared profile
1. Click **"Import Preset..."**.
2. Select the file you received.
3. The profile is added to your list. If one with the same name exists, the new one is renamed (for example "FastGT3 (1)").
